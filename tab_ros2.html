📖 Physical Odometry Calibration Procedures

1. Straight 1-Meter Calibration Test:
• Align rover rear bumper with start line.
• Execute recording helper on RPi5:
ros2 run rover_bringup record_odom_calibration
• Move rover straight forward by exactly 1.000m to target line.
• Compare physical distance vs reported /odom displacement.
2. 90-Degree Turn Calibration Test:
• Align rover chassis with orientation mark.
• Record starting /odom yaw pose.
• Turn rover 90 degrees CCW (or CW) in place.
• Verify reported yaw change equals 1.5708 rad (90.0°).
